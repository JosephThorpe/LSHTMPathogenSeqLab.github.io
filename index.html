What we do

We are an interdisciplinary group working to understand infectious diseases, with a focus on tuberculosis, malaria and antimicrobial-resistant (AMR) infections. We combine 'omics, laboratory experiments and big data science to inform decisions in public health, clinical practice and surveillance.

We also help build genomic capacity in endemic settings. Through collaborative projects and hands-on workshops in 'omics and bioinformatics, we train researchers and healthcare professionals and grow a network that strengthens our shared response to infectious disease.
